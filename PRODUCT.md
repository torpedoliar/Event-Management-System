# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

**Primary: Event Organizers & Staff**
Admins managing registration desks, running check-in operations, and orchestrating live moments (lucky draws, tournaments, prize announcements). They operate under time pressure at events — setup before, execution during. Need confidence that every guest interaction will be smooth.

**Secondary: Event Guests**
Attendees registering or confirming their attendance. Interact briefly — scan a QR, fill a form, claim a souvenir. They expect elegance without friction; a clunky check-in feels like a cheap event.

## Product Purpose

A complete event management system that handles the full lifecycle: guest registration, QR-based check-in, live lucky draws with big-screen display, tournament bracket management, and souvenir distribution. The system must feel premium from the moment a guest scans the QR — it sets the tone for the entire event.

## Positioning

**Premium event experiences, effortlessly executed.**

Elegance without complexity. Every screen — from the admin dashboard to the guest registration form to the winners display — reinforces that this is a well-run, professional event.

## Brand Personality

**Professional & Premium** — The visual language says "this event took care of every detail." Heritage gold accents on a dark foundation signal sophistication. Nothing feels rushed or generic.

Voice: Confident, warm, polished. Not corporate-cold. Not startup-casual.

## Anti-references

**Explicitly NOT:**
- Generic SaaS dashboards with blue/purple gradients and "freemium" energy
- Budget event software with clip art, dated UI patterns, or visual clutter
- Over-designed interfaces that sacrifice clarity for decoration
- Anything that feels "AI-generated" — no gradient text, no eyebrow sections, no numbered sequence scaffolding

## Design Principles

1. **Premium without pretension** — Rich colors and elegant typography serve clarity, not ego
2. **Confidence in interaction** — Every tap, click, and transition should feel intentional and responsive
3. **Hype moments matter** — Lucky draws and winner displays are theatrical; they deserve drama and celebration
4. **The details are the brand** — Placeholder text, loading states, error messages — all reflect care or its absence
5. **Dark theme for the room** — Event venues are dim; the UI adapts to the environment, not the office

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AA minimum; strive for AAA on critical paths (check-in, registration)
- Reduced motion support is non-negotiable — guests with vestibular disorders must have full access
- High contrast ratios: body text ≥4.5:1, large text ≥3:1
- Color is never the only signal — icons, patterns, and text labels accompany all status indicators
